New Zealand age-group records

The record book.

Individual age-group records only, ages 13-18. Joint record holders each receive one record. Outstanding-record margins compare each holder with the fastest different swimmer.

Methodology. Margin = (next-fastest time - record time) / next-fastest time. A swimmer's own previous record is not an eligible comparator. Joint records therefore have a 0% margin to the other joint holder. Top-20 tables use named/verified comparators.

Who holds the most?

Different pace of recent change

Male: 76 of 203 dated, swimmer-held records (37.4%) were set in the past two years. Female: 33 of 209 dated, swimmer-held records (15.8%) were set in the past two years. This is a snapshot of surviving current records, not the total number broken in each year.

Male diagonal: successive age groups

Historical diagonal candidates include Ariel Muchirahondo and Grayson Coulter; select adjacent age-row cells to confirm which record times and holders still survive. As the record book changes, the strength and dates of these diagonal bands can change.

Female diagonals: earlier generations

Historical diagonal candidates include Erika Fairweather and Gabrielle Fa'amausili. Check the age, swimmer and date details in the cells: diagonals suggest cohorts progressing across ages, not proof that one swimmer set every record in a band.

Recent female records are concentrated at older ages

Compare the recent female rows with earlier-age rows to see where the pace of record changes is concentrated. Zoe Pedersen, Milan Glintmeyer and Monique Wieruszowski are useful examples to explore, but the distribution should be reassessed at every refresh. Interpretation: individual heat-map cells do not prove a swimmer's progression without checking dates and names.

Methodology: one current event/course/age record per cell, including ties once (not once per joint holder); original date for equal record times. Unclaimed “Standard” records are excluded. The 10–15, 15–20 and 20+ year columns aggregate broader periods than the first ten annual columns. Older records are not adjusted for changes in events or competition conditions.
