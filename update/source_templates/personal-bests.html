About the comparisons

Reading the gaps.

Gap = (personal best ÷ published standard − 1) × 100. A positive number means the PB is slower than the entry standard. A negative number means it is faster. Display is rounded to one decimal place; calculations use hundredths of a second. These standards apply to open-age world championships, not age-group competitions.

Long-course A/B benchmarks are for Budapest 2027 (50m); short-course A/B benchmarks are for Beijing 2026 (25m). They are different competition standards. Official qualification also requires a swim in an approved meet during the specified qualifying period and selection by the federation; the presence of a faster historical PB alone does not establish eligibility.

Personal bests include eligible individual swims and legal relay lead-offs, merged across both Grayson SwimCloud profiles. Where a PB was achieved more than once, the first recorded date is displayed. Figures are calculated from swims loaded in the master workbook as at 10 October 2026; this page updates only after the acquisition and rebuild process is run.
